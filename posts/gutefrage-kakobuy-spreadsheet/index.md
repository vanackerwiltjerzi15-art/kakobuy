## How to Use the Kakobuy Spreadsheet: Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Download and Setup

1. Access the spreadsheet template from Kakobuy
2. Make a copy to your Google Drive or download as Excel file
3. Review all tabs and columns to understand the structure
4. Customize categories based on your product niche

### Step 2: Product Research Entry

For each product you're researching, enter:

- **Product name and description**
- **Supplier name and contact information**
- **Product URL** (1688, Alibaba, Taobao, etc.)
- **Base price** at different MOQ levels
- **Shipping cost** to your location
- **Estimated delivery time**

### Step 3: Calculate Key Metrics

The spreadsheet automatically calculates:

- **Landed cost per unit** (product + shipping + duties)
- **Profit margin** at different selling prices
- **Break-even point** (minimum units to sell)
- **ROI** (Return on Investment) percentage

### Step 4: Supplier Comparison

Use the comparison tab to:

- Rank suppliers by total score
- Identify best value (not just lowest price)
- Track communication history
- Note payment terms and conditions

### Step 5: Order Management

Once you decide to order:

- Update order status (Sample ordered, Bulk order placed, Shipped, Delivered)
- Track actual costs vs. estimated
- Record quality inspection results
- Document any issues for future reference

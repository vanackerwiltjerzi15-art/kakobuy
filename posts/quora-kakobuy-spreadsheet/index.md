**Quora user James T. from London reports:**

> "Garbage in, garbage out. I spent 2 weeks just cleaning up my initial data. Now my decisions are 10x better because I'm working with accurate information."

- ✅ Verify all supplier quotes in writing
- ✅ Include shipping costs in landed cost calculations
- ✅ Update prices regularly (they change frequently)
- ✅ Track actual vs. estimated costs
- ✅ Document all communications with suppliers

## Common Mistakes Quora Users Warn About

### Mistake #1: Not Including All Costs

**Warning from Quora veteran Lisa R.:**

> "My first spreadsheet only had product costs. I forgot about shipping, customs, insurance, and payment processing fees. My 'profitable' product actually lost money!"

**Complete Cost Checklist:**
- Product unit cost
- Shipping (air/sea/express)
- Customs duties and taxes
- Insurance
- Payment processing fees (PayPal, wire transfer)
- Quality inspection costs
- Warehousing (if applicable)
- Marketing and advertising

### Mistake #2: Ignoring MOQ Implications

**Quora contributor Ahmed S. explains:**

> "Higher MOQ means lower unit cost, but also higher risk. I learned to balance this carefully."

**MOQ Decision Framework:**

| Scenario | Recommended Approach |
|----------|---------------------|
| Testing new product | Order minimum quantity, accept higher unit cost |
| Proven seller | Increase MOQ for better margins |
| Seasonal items | Match MOQ to single season demand |
| Evergreen products | Higher MOQ for best pricing |

### Mistake #3: Poor Supplier Documentation

**Quora user Rachel W. advises:**

> "I thought I'd remember which supplier offered what. Wrong! Now I document everything: chat logs, quotes, samples, certifications."

**Supplier Documentation System:**
1. Save all email communications
2. Screenshot chat conversations
3. Store product specifications
4. Keep sample photos
5. Archive certificates and test reports

## FAQ: Quora's Most Asked Questions

### Q1: Is the Kakobuy spreadsheet better than paid tools?

**A:** It depends on your needs. The spreadsheet is:
- ✅ Better for: Beginners, small budgets, customization lovers
- ❌ Less ideal for: Those wanting automation, large teams, advanced integrations

Many Quora users start with the spreadsheet and upgrade to paid tools only when their business justifies the cost.

### Q2: How do I handle multiple currencies?

**A:** Add columns for:
- Original currency price
- Exchange rate used
- Converted price (USD/EUR/GBP)
- Exchange rate fluctuation buffer (5-10%)

### Q3: Can multiple team members use it simultaneously?

**A:** Yes! Host it on Google Sheets for real-time collaboration. Set permissions:

- Admin access for business owners

# Yeezy 350 Granite Review: Quality & Fit Guide

[← Back to Home](/) · [Browse Archives](/posts/)

![Yeezy 350 Granite review and quality assessment by Kakobuy](/image/yeezy-350-granite-review.webp)

The Yeezy Boost 350 Granite brings together the signature comfort of Boost technology with a versatile neutral colorway that works with virtually any outfit. Designed by Kanye West in collaboration with Adidas, the 350 silhouette has become one of the most influential sneaker designs of the 2010s. The Granite colorway features a muted grey-toned Primeknit upper that offers understated elegance. In this review, we break down everything you need to know.

## 1. Design & Aesthetics

The Yeezy 350 Granite features a sleek, low-profile silhouette with a Primeknit upper in a granite grey tone. The signature side stripe runs along the midfoot, and the translucent monofilament side panel adds visual depth. The sculpted Boost midsole creates a distinctive profile that's instantly recognizable. The neutral grey colorway makes it one of the most versatile Yeezy releases, easily pairing with both casual and elevated outfits.

## 2. Quality & Materials

The Primeknit upper is soft, breathable, and stretches to conform to your foot shape. The knit construction eliminates traditional seams, reducing potential irritation points. The full-length Boost midsole delivers exceptional energy return and all-day comfort—the Boost technology remains one of the most comfortable cushioning systems in sneakers. The rubber outsole provides adequate grip for everyday use, though it's not designed for intense athletic activity.

## 3. Fit & Sizing

Yeezy 350s are known to run small. It's widely recommended to go up half a size from your usual sneaker size for a comfortable fit. The Primeknit upper does stretch slightly with wear, but the initial snugness can be uncomfortable if you stick with your true size. The sock-like construction hugs the foot, and the pull tab at the heel makes them easy to slip on and off.

## 4. Value for Money

The Yeezy 350 Granite offers strong value for a sneaker with this level of comfort technology and design pedigree. The Boost midsole alone justifies the price, as it delivers comfort that few competitors can match. The neutral colorway ensures the shoe remains relevant season after season, making it a long-term wardrobe investment.

## 5. Community Feedback

The Kakobuy community rates the Yeezy 350 Granite highly for its unmatched comfort and versatile styling. Members consistently mention it as their go-to shoe for long days on their feet. The Granite colorway is praised for being easier to keep clean than lighter Yeezy colorways while still maintaining that neutral aesthetic. It's frequently recommended for anyone prioritizing comfort in their sneaker rotation.

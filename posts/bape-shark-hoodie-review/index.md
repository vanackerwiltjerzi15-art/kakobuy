# BAPE Shark Hoodie Review: Quality & Fit Guide

[← Back to Home](/) · [Browse Archives](/posts/)

![BAPE Shark Hoodie review and quality assessment by Kakobuy](/image/bape-shark-hoodie-review.webp)

The BAPE Shark Hoodie is arguably the most iconic piece from A Bathing Ape, the legendary Japanese streetwear brand founded by Nigo in 1993. With its distinctive full-zip hood that covers the entire face when zipped up, featuring the shark's mouth and eyes, this hoodie has become a symbol of streetwear culture worldwide. Worn by everyone from Pharrell to Travis Scott, the Shark Hoodie remains a grail piece for collectors and enthusiasts. In this review, we break down the quality, fit, and value.

## 1. Design & Aesthetics

The Shark Hoodie's design is unmistakable. The full-zip hood extends all the way to the top, revealing the iconic shark face graphic with sharp teeth and menacing eyes when fully zipped. The front features BAPE's signature camo pattern and the ABC logo. The design is bold, attention-grabbing, and instantly recognizable. Available in numerous colorways—from classic green camo to solid blacks and seasonal collaborations—each version maintains the signature shark aesthetic.

## 2. Quality & Materials

Constructed from a heavyweight cotton fleece, the Shark Hoodie feels substantial and premium. The fabric is thick enough to provide warmth while remaining comfortable for layering. The screen-printed graphics are vibrant and detailed, with the shark face being particularly well-executed. The zipper is a heavy-duty YKK that operates smoothly even when the hood is fully zipped. Ribbed cuffs and hem are thick and maintain their shape. The overall construction reflects BAPE's commitment to quality manufacturing.

## 3. Fit & Sizing

BAPE hoodies generally run slightly smaller than Western brands, so it's recommended to size up for a comfortable fit. The Shark Hoodie has a regular-to-slightly-boxy cut. The full-zip hood is the standout feature—when zipped fully, it creates the signature shark face look. The hood is well-structured and doesn't collapse when not zipped. Sleeve length is standard, and the body length sits at the hip.

## 4. Value for Money

The BAPE Shark Hoodie sits at the premium end of streetwear pricing, but its iconic status and cultural significance justify the investment. It's a piece that has maintained its relevance for over two decades, and the quality of construction ensures it will last. On the resale market, certain colorways and collaborations can command significantly higher prices, making some versions appreciating assets.

## 5. Community Feedback

The Kakobuy community considers the BAPE Shark Hoodie a cornerstone piece for any serious streetwear collection. Members praise the bold design, heavyweight construction, and the way it instantly elevates any outfit. The full-zip hood feature is consistently highlighted as a unique selling point. It's frequently recommended as a statement piece that pairs well with simple bottoms to let the hoodie be the focal point.

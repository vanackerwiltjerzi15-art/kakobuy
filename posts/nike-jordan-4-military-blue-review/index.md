# Nike Jordan 4 Military Blue Review: Quality & Fit Guide

[← Back to Home](/) · [Browse Archives](/posts/)

![Nike Jordan 4 Military Blue review and quality assessment by Kakobuy](/image/nike-jordan-4-military-blue-review.webp)

The Nike Jordan 4 Military Blue is one of the most sought-after retro releases in the Jordan Brand lineup. Originally dropping in 1989 and reissued multiple times, this colorway combines a clean white leather upper with Military Blue accents on the midsole, eyelets, and heel tab. In this review, we break down everything you need to know about quality, fit, and whether it lives up to the hype.

## 1. Design & Aesthetics

The Military Blue colorway is a timeless classic. The crisp white tumbled leather base is complemented by pops of Military Blue on the visible Air unit, wing eyelets, and heel tab. The signature mesh panels on the quarter and tongue add breathability and visual depth. The Nike Air branding on the heel stays true to the original 1989 design, making this a grail for retro Jordan collectors.

## 2. Quality & Materials

The upper features premium tumbled leather that feels soft yet durable out of the box. The mesh panels are tightly woven with no loose threads. The midsole paint is clean with minimal factory imperfections. Overall build quality is solid, with consistent stitching throughout and well-bonded materials. The visible Air unit in the heel provides both cushioning and a signature aesthetic touch.

## 3. Fit & Sizing

Jordan 4s generally fit true to size. If you have wider feet, consider going up half a size for a more comfortable fit. The toe box has adequate room, and the padded collar provides good ankle support. Break-in time is minimal—most wearers find them comfortable from day one.

## 4. Value for Money

At retail, the Jordan 4 Military Blue offers excellent value for a retro sneaker with this level of heritage and quality. On the resale market, prices vary depending on size and condition, but the quality-to-price ratio remains strong compared to other retro Jordans in the same tier.

## 5. Community Feedback

The Kakobuy community consistently rates the Jordan 4 Military Blue highly for its clean colorway and versatile styling. Members praise its ability to pair with both casual and elevated outfits. Common feedback highlights the accurate retro detailing and comfortable wear experience.
